Pathwise estimation requires a differentiable sampling transform and objective. It often reduces variance, but there is no universal variance ordering for all distributions and objectives. Ordinary continuous pathwise gradients do not directly apply to categories; relaxations and straight-through estimators introduce their own tradeoffs.
